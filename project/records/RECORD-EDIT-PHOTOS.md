# Review photo removal and splitting by selection

BROWSE-PHOTOS resolved the old viewing/access decision. RECORD-SCREEN shows
full-screen photographs. COLLECTION-PHOTO-FOLLOWUP handles additional photos
and specific requests from the existing collection. This record now covers
only the remaining operations.

- Remove a mistaken photograph without losing evidence supporting readings.
- Split one entry depicting two physical records by selecting the photos
  belonging to each, preserving identity, capture provenance and audit.

The desk split tool exists. A user-facing workflow needs a concrete review
of the resulting records and explicit confirmation before destructive or
schema-altering operations. Do not infer permission to remove evidence from
a request to add a better photograph. PHOTO-CULL owns wider culling policy.
