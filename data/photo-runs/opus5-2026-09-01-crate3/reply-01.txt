Notes on the two judgement calls, so they are visible rather than buried:

485 — the only alphanumeric on the sleeve is GSTP-8A/S8, bottom-left of
the back, and it is NOT labelled as a catalogue number. It also visibly
encodes a disc-and-side ("8A/S8") on a set whose front carries a bare
"8" and whose back says RECORD EIGHT. Left unassigned rather than
guessed.

486 — the sleeve spine reads "MONO—AN 133-4"; the two disc labels read
"AN. 133" (side 1) and "AN. 134" (side 4). Reported the set number as
printed on the sleeve and put both disc numbers in other_numbers.
XAX.2621 and XAX.2624 are stamped beside the spindle and are not
presented as catalogue numbers.

484 — sleeve only, no disc label among the six photographs. Nothing on
it carries a year; 1884 appears in the liner prose about Hanson, not as
a date for this pressing.
