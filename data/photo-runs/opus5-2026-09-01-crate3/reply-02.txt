Notes:

487 — three forms of one number appear: "M. 2316" inside the Concert
Hall wreath on the front, "AM 2316" stamped on the back beside a
handwritten signature, and "AM-2316" on the disc label itself. Took the
disc's form as the catalogue number and left the front-cover variant in
other_numbers, since only the disc says which is in the sleeve.

488 — sleeve only; no disc label among the six photographs. "2578 008"
is bottom-right of the back sleeve, unlabelled but alone and in
catalogue format. This is the same shape of call I refused on 485, and
the difference is that 2578 008 encodes nothing but itself.

489 — the back sleeve advertises four OTHER records with their own
numbers (CLP 72101, CLP 72110, CGLP 75706, SCGLP 75707). They are in
other_numbers, not near catno_raw. 33-061 044 is printed on the disc
label opposite CGLX 73717 and is not presented as the catalogue number.

Rotations on the sleeve shots (488-3, 488-4, 489-1) are lower
confidence than the disc labels: I derived them from line order rather
than glyph direction. The disc-label calls in reply-01 (486-4, 486-5 at
180) are unambiguous.
